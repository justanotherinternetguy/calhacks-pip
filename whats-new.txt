WHAT'S NEW (commit 88fa3cb, 2026-11-17)

The app now centers on friends checking in on each other.
Passive detection is a backup. The AI risk score is gone.


1. FRIEND CHECK-INS
   - A friend taps "You okay?". You get a notification that stays
     until you answer.
   - Answer "I'm OK" (unlock needed) or "I need help" (starts SOS).
   - No answer in 10 minutes: your group gets an alert.
   - Limits: group-mates only, one open check-in at a time,
     2-minute wait between asks.

2. NOTIFICATIONS THAT STAY PUT (Android)
   - Check-in and alert notifications come back if swiped away
     or after a reboot.
   - They clear on every phone once the issue is resolved.

3. JOIN GROUPS IN PERSON ONLY
   - You join by scanning a friend's QR code.
   - Each code works once and expires after 10 minutes.
   - Both phones confirm each other's fingerprints.
   - Max 12 people per group.

4. KEY CHECKS
   - Each friend's key is saved the first time it's seen.
   - If it changes, that friend is left out until you re-check
     them in person.

5. ENCRYPTED LIVE LOCATION
   - Live location is always encrypted. Only your friends can read it.
   - The key changes daily and whenever someone leaves the group.

6. REMOVED: AI RISK ANALYSIS
   - The AI score, its timeline and its pop-up reminders are gone.
   - No AI keys ship in the web app anymore.
